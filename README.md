# In-silico Characterization of ATP7B Variants Associated with Wilson Disease

## Overview

This project investigates genetic variants in the human ATP7B gene associated with Wilson disease using publicly available genomic, clinical, protein, and population-level resources.

## Research Question

What are the characteristics of ATP7B variants associated with Wilson disease, and what can computational analysis reveal about their potential functional significance?

## Objectives

- Retrieve reference information for the human ATP7B gene and protein.
- Identify and curate ATP7B variants associated with Wilson disease.
- Classify variants according to their molecular consequences.
- Examine clinical annotations and population frequencies.
- Analyze protein domains and sequence conservation.
- Explore the potential structural significance of selected variants.
- Visualize and interpret the resulting data.
